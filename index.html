AD

Learn Fintech – Adyen's Unified Commerce Architecture

Why Adyen conquered global payments: replacing 4 legacy intermediaries with a single platform:

Single Gateway vs Legacy Fragmentation

Traditional setups string together a gateway, risk manager, and merchant acquirer across multiple vendors. Adyen collapsed this entire stack into a single unified API, minimizing transaction latency and boosting authorization rates.

Interchange++ Transparent Pricing

Instead of opaque blended fee structures, Interchange++ passes true card scheme fees (Visa/Mastercard) and issuing bank interchange directly with a transparent fixed Adyen markup, saving enterprise merchants millions in overhead.

LP

A Lending App Journey (Unsecured Retail Prototype)

Based on my academic project: "Studied fintech business models and scalability, developed an unsecured retail lending prototype covering digital onboarding, KYC, credit assessment and loan disbursement":

Stage 1
Frictionless Digital Onboarding & e-KYC

Aadhaar XML & PAN API verification in under 45 seconds, paired with passive liveness detection to eliminate identity fraud.

Stage 2
Alternative Credit Underwriting Engine

Parsing bank statements via Account Aggregator frameworks + calculating debt-to-income and cashflow volatility beyond rigid CIBIL thresholds.

Stage 3
Automated Risk Scoring & Loan Sanction

Instant credit line recommendation, personalized risk-adjusted APR calculation, and e-mandate (eNACH) auto-debit registration.

Stage 4
Instant Disbursal & Real-Time Monitoring

Immediate IMPS / UPI disbursal to borrower account, linked with early-warning delinquency triggers and automated repayment reminders.
